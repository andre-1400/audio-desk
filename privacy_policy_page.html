Audio Desk

Privacy Policy

Audio Desk is a macOS menu bar app that shows what's playing in Spotify or Apple Music, right on your desktop. This page explains — plainly — what that involves.

The short version

Audio Desk does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising in this app.

How it actually works

To show track info and let you control playback, Audio Desk talks directly to the Spotify and Apple Music apps already on your Mac, using Apple's built-in Automation (Apple Events) framework. That conversation:

  • happens entirely on your own device
  • is never sent to Audio Desk's developer, or anyone else
  • is never written to disk — it only lives in memory while the app is running

macOS will ask you to approve an Automation permission the first time Audio Desk needs it. That permission is what lets the app read the current track and send play, pause, skip, and seek commands on your behalf — nothing more.

Third parties

Audio Desk does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-processing service.

Children's privacy

Audio Desk doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13 — because it doesn't collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it. Continuing to use Audio Desk after an update means you accept the revised policy.
