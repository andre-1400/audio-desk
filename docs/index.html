Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the widget go after I close the setup window?

The setup window closing doesn't quit the app — look for the note icon (♪) in your menu bar. Click it any time to bring the gallery back.

Can I move the widget?

Yes — just click and drag it anywhere on your desktop.

How do I change styles or settings?

Right-click the widget itself, or right-click the menu bar icon, to switch styles, hide the widget, or open Settings.

Which music apps does it support?

Audio Desk reads now-playing info from Apple Music and Spotify.

Does Audio Desk collect my data?

No. Audio Desk runs entirely on your Mac and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.
