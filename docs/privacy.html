Privacy Policy

Audio Desk for macOS · Last updated August 19, 2026

Audio Desk does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It runs entirely on your Mac.

What Audio Desk Does

Audio Desk displays the currently playing track from Apple Music or Spotify as a desktop widget. To do this, it reads now-playing information (track name, artist, album art, and playback position) directly from those apps on your own Mac, using Apple's standard scripting interfaces.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not collect any information about you or your device.
  • We do not transmit any data to our servers — we don't operate any servers that receive app data.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs of any kind.
  • We do not share data with third parties, because none is collected in the first place.
  • We do not require an account, sign-in, or network connection to use the app.

Local Data Only

Any preferences you set (widget style, size, position, colors) are stored locally on your Mac using standard macOS application preferences (UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device.

Album Artwork

When available, album artwork is retrieved directly from the music app you're using (Apple Music or Spotify) or from Apple's public artwork lookup, solely to display it in the widget. This artwork is not stored or transmitted anywhere beyond your own screen.

Children's Privacy

Audio Desk does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date above.
