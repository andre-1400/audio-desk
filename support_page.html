Common questions

Audio Desk isn't showing my current track.
Make sure Spotify or Apple Music is open and playing, and that Audio Desk has been granted Automation permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation.

Does Audio Desk collect any of my data?
No — everything happens locally on your Mac. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Is this a subscription?
No — Audio Desk is a one-time purchase. No subscriptions, ever.
