# Privacy Policy for Audio Desk

_Last updated: August 18, 2026_

Audio Desk is a macOS menu bar application that displays now-playing information and playback controls for Spotify and Apple Music directly on your desktop.

## Data Collection

Audio Desk does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services of any kind.

## How Audio Desk Works

To display track information and control playback, Audio Desk communicates locally and directly with the Spotify and Apple Music applications already installed on your Mac, using Apple's built-in Apple Events (AppleScript) automation framework. This communication:

- Happens entirely on your own device
- Is never sent to Audio Desk's developer or any third party
- Is never stored outside of your Mac's local memory while the app is running

Audio Desk requests Automation permission (via macOS System Settings) solely to read the currently playing track and send playback commands (play, pause, skip, seek) to Spotify or Apple Music on your behalf.

## Third-Party Services

Audio Desk does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-processing services.

## Children's Privacy

Audio Desk does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
