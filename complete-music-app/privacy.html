Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

MusicWidgetss is a simple macOS menu‑bar app sold by an individual developer based in Spain. This policy explains, plainly, what data is collected when you visit this website or buy the app, and what isn't.

What this website collects

This website (musicwidgetss.netlify.app) is a static page. It does not use cookies, analytics scripts, or any third‑party tracking. Browsing this site does not create any record tied to you.

What happens when you buy MusicWidgetss

Payment is handled entirely by Stripe, our payment processor. When you purchase, Stripe collects your email address (to send you a receipt) and your payment details (card number, etc.) directly — that information passes through Stripe's systems, never through ours. We never see or store your card details.

We do receive, from Stripe, the email address you used to pay and the fact that a payment was made, so we can respond if you contact us about a purchase. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not sell, rent, or share this information with anyone.

What the app itself does

MusicWidgetss runs locally on your Mac. It reads the "now playing" information (track title, artist, album art) from Spotify or Apple Music on your own device, in order to display it on the desktop widget. It does not require an account, does not send your listening activity anywhere, and does not communicate with our servers at all. Nothing about what you play is collected, transmitted, or seen by us.

Data retention

Payment records (email, transaction amount, date) are retained by Stripe per their own retention policy and by us only as far as needed for accounting/tax obligations under Spanish law. We don't keep any other personal data.

Your rights

Since you're likely in the EU, under GDPR you have the right to ask what data we hold about you (in practice: your email and purchase record, as also visible in your Stripe receipt) and to request its deletion, subject to our legal obligation to keep tax/accounting records for the period required by Spanish law. To make a request, email us — see below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change too. Material changes will be reflected directly on this page.
