One honest thing, before you buy.

MusicWidgetss is built by one person, not a big studio — and it isn't notarized by Apple yet. Notarization costs a yearly developer fee I haven't been able to put down just yet. So the very first time you open the app, macOS will show you a security warning.

It looks scarier than it is. Your Mac isn't in any danger — it just takes ten seconds to click past it (full steps are on your download page), and after that first time, MusicWidgetss opens normally, forever. I'd rather tell you this now than have it surprise you after you've already paid.

Questions

Good to know.

Does it work with both Spotify and Apple Music?

Yes. MusicWidgetss reads the local now‑playing information from whichever app is active. No logins, no API keys, no internet required for core features.

Is this a subscription?

No. It's a single €2.99 one‑time purchase. You own it forever — no recurring fees, ever.

Why does macOS show a warning when I open it?

Because MusicWidgetss isn't notarized by Apple yet — that's a paid yearly program I haven't been able to join yet as a one‑person project. The warning looks scary, but it's normal for independently‑made apps, and it's not a sign anything is actually wrong. You'll just need to right‑click the app and choose "Open" once (full steps are on your download page) — after that first time, it opens normally forever.

Will the widget get in the way of my work?

Never. The widget floats quietly behind your apps and above your wallpaper, and you can drag it wherever you like. The app itself lives in your menu bar.

What macOS version do I need?

macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. Built natively in Swift / SwiftUI.

Can I switch between Apple Music and Spotify looks?

Yes. You pick your theme when you first open the app — a clean Apple Music white‑and‑red, or a dark Spotify black‑and‑green — and you can change it anytime.

How many widgets show at once?

You show one widget on your desktop at a time, and you can switch between any vinyl or CD style whenever you feel like a change.
